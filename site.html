How it works

Three steps, done while you sleep.

No plugins. No browser extensions. Just connect your Gmail and let it run.

01

Connect Gmail

Paste your Gmail address and a 16-char app password (generated from your Google account, revokable anytime). We connect via IMAP read-only — we physically cannot send or delete anything.

02

AI triages everything

Each email gets sorted: Customer inquiry, Sales lead, High-risk item, or Spam/Promo. Customers get a reply draft ready to copy-paste-edit-send. Deadlines get pulled into a task list.

03

You review and act

Open your daily report. Handle the 2–3 emails that matter. Ignore the 27 that don't. Copy a draft, tweak one line, hit send. Done in under 5 minutes.

Four buckets

Every email lands in exactly one place.

You open the two that matter. The rest is already handled.

HIGH RISK

Read this yourself

Chargebacks, disputes, legal language, account warnings. Flagged red. No auto-drafts. Your call, always.

CUSTOMER

Someone is waiting

Order questions, shipping, sizing, returns. Each comes with a reply draft attached — copy, edit, send.

LEAD

Money on the table

Wholesale requests, collabs, custom quotes. Surfaced first so you answer while they're still warm.

SPAM / PROMO

Safely ignored

Newsletters, cold outreach, platform noise. Filed away so it stops competing for your attention.

Boundaries

What it deliberately does not do.

  • ✕ Never sends email automatically. Drafts only — you read, you edit, you hit send.
  • ✕ Never negotiates contracts, disputes, or money issues. It flags them and steps back.
  • ✕ Not an enterprise helpdesk. One person, one Gmail, one inbox. That's the whole scope.
  • ✕ Doesn't store your emails long-term. Results are generated fresh each run.
